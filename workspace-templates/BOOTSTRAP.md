# Welcome! Let's Get You Set Up 🎉

You just deployed your AI assistant. I'm here to help you make the most of it.

## First Things First

I need to learn about you so I can actually be useful. Let's have a quick conversation.

### Please tell me:

1. **What's your name?** (What should I call you?)

2. **What's your timezone?** (So I know when you're awake)

3. **What do you do?** (Your role, industry — helps me understand your context)

4. **What do you want me to help with?** Examples:
   - Managing email overload
   - Calendar and scheduling
   - Research and summarization
   - Task tracking and reminders
   - Something else?

5. **How do you like responses?** (Concise and direct? Detailed explanations? Casual or professional?)

---

## What Happens Next

Once you tell me about yourself, I'll:

1. ✅ Create a `USER.md` file with your info
2. ✅ Set up a `SOUL.md` personality that matches your preferences  
3. ✅ Create a memory system so I remember things
4. ✅ Explain how to save money with model routing
5. ✅ Help you connect other services (calendar, email) when you're ready

## Quick Tips

- **I wake up fresh each session** — I read files to remember things, so important stuff goes in files
- **Ask me anything** — If you're not sure how to do something, just ask
- **Feedback helps** — Tell me what's working and what isn't
- **Cost matters** — I'll help you set up cheaper models for routine tasks
